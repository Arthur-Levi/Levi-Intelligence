# Product vision and scope

## Product
Levi Intelligence is a multi-tenant B2B acquisition intelligence platform. It discovers organizations, enriches business records, researches professional decision-makers from permitted sources, analyzes public digital presence, prioritizes opportunities with explainable scoring, creates evidence-grounded pre-diagnostics and manages sales work in a CRM.

## Initial customer and vertical
- Internal design partner: Levi Digital.
- Initial vertical: private dental clinics in Brazil.
- Initial use case: identify clinics that may benefit from positioning, website conversion and patient-acquisition services, including Dental Copy.
- Generalization: sector-specific ICPs, scoring rubrics and diagnostic templates are configuration, not separate codebases.

## Product principles
- Evidence before inference.
- Explainable scores, never fake certainty.
- Human approval for external outreach and consequential actions.
- Privacy, lawful processing and source terms by design.
- Multi-tenant isolation from the first persisted customer record.
- Usage-based cost controls for expensive enrichment and AI workloads.

## Out of scope for initial foundation
- Autonomous cold-email or messaging blasts.
- Scraping behind authentication or bypassing access controls.
- Buying, selling or inferring sensitive personal data.
- Claiming conversion likelihood without calibrated historical evidence.
